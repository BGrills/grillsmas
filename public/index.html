👋 Getting started

  1. Use the family password to get in (you only need it once per device).
  2. Click “You are:” at the top and pick your name. Everything you add goes under that name.
  3. On a shared computer or iPad? Switch names before you add anything.

📅 The calendar

  1. Go to Who's Where.
  2. Under My trip, enter the day you arrive in Whistler and the day you leave. Press Add my trip. Your column turns icy blue for those days.
  3. Busy on certain days? Under Block off time, pick a date (or a range), choose Daytime, Evening or All day, and give it a name if you want, like “Ski day”.
  4. The 🏔️ Here column shows how many of us are in town each day. A 🎉 means everyone's here.
  5. Made a mistake? Tap your own chip in the calendar, or use the ✖ in My stuff, to remove it.

🎁 Wish lists

  1. Go to Wish Lists and fill in Add to my list. A link and notes (size, colour) help a lot.
  2. Shopping for someone? Find their stocking and press 🎁 I'll get this! on what you're buying. Everyone else sees it's taken.
  3. Changed your mind? Press Un-claim.
  4. You'll never see what's been claimed on your own list. No peeking!

🍪 Food requests

  1. Go to Food Requests.
  2. Type what you'd love in Chantelle's Bake Shop (treats) or Chef Brendan's Dinner Menu (dinners). Pick who's asking, and press Request it.
  3. The chef marks each one ✅ yes, 🤔 maybe or 🙅 no. Bribes are not officially accepted.
  4. You can delete your own requests. The chef can delete anything on their menu.
